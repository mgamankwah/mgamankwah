From mathematical discovery to real-world impact.

I am an applied mathematician and data scientist driven by curiosity, complex problem-solving, and the opportunity to translate ideas into meaningful impact.

My academic journey began in Ghana, where I studied mathematics and scientific computing before pursuing a PhD in Applied Mathematics at Case Western Reserve University. Along the way, I explored diverse scientific challenges, from Bayesian inverse problems and human biomechanics to quantum algorithms and advanced computational methods.

Research experiences at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, NERSC, and IonQ broadened my perspective on how mathematics, computing, and emerging technologies can address complex problems across disciplines.

Today, at Dell Technologies, I bring this scientific foundation into an enterprise environment, contributing to modernization and AI adoption initiatives that connect technical innovation with practical business needs.

What particularly interests me is the intersection of technology, strategy, and execution—understanding not only how a solution works, but why it matters, how it can be implemented effectively, and what value it creates.

How I approach the work

I start by asking what we need to understand and what decision the work should support. Scientific rigor means testing assumptions, examining uncertainty and being clear about limitations. Strategic thinking means connecting those findings to priorities and the realities of implementation.

I value collaboration throughout that process: listening to different perspectives, making technical ideas understandable and helping a team move from analysis to useful action.
