From research to enterprise data science

I’m a data scientist and applied mathematician with a PhD in Applied Mathematics. My work at Dell Technologies brings data science, automation, and emerging AI capabilities into supply chain operations and business decision-making.

My scientific work spans Bayesian statistics, uncertainty quantification, machine learning, scientific computing, and quantum computing. During my doctorate I studied how muscles are recruited during movement. Research internships at Berkeley Lab, NERSC, and IonQ broadened my experience in quantum computing, and my work at Outlier brought me into language model evaluation and advanced mathematical reasoning.

I’m interested in how organizations can translate complex problems into practical solutions and how AI can deliver lasting business value.
