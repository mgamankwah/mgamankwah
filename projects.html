Team project · 2025

Alzheimer’s Disease Risk Prediction

As part of an Erdos Institute team, I contributed exploratory analysis and feature-importance notebooks. Our team compared predictive models, examined feature importance, and calibrated probability estimates for risk assessment.

The project used synthetic data for exploratory modeling and did not establish clinical validity.

Team project · Environmental data

ChloroFish

Our team used Southeast Area Monitoring and Assessment Program data to study the relationship between chlorophyll and fishery measures. The project compared statistical and machine learning models, including neural networks, and explored prediction intervals for shrimp catch.

My documented contribution includes preparing a modified shrimp dataset.

Doctoral research · Collaborative publications

MYOBOLICA

In my doctoral research, I used Bayesian inference to investigate which muscle recruitment patterns could explain observed movement and how uncertainty in those estimates changes over time. The related MYOBOLICA study describes a stochastic approach for estimating variability in muscle control.

Collaborative research · Quantum computing

Quantum Pixel Representation and Compression

Our research team developed quantum representations for multidimensional images and studied how image compression could reduce the resources needed for those representations. The published work describes the algorithms and compares examples at different compression levels.

Team project · Public data

Food Environment Atlas

Our team used U.S. Department of Agriculture data to model county-level diabetes prevalence using food access and socioeconomic indicators. The analysis related county characteristics to reported prevalence at the population level.
