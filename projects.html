Team project · 2025 · Synthetic data

Alzheimer’s Disease Risk Prediction

Python · scikit-learn · XGBoost · Optuna

How can a model distinguish diagnoses while making its predictions easier to interpret?

Problem & approach
Our team compared classification models on synthetic records, tuned hyperparameters and examined feature importance. We also evaluated isotonic and sigmoid probability calibration for exploratory risk assessment.
My contribution
I contributed exploratory data analysis and feature-importance notebooks. Model optimization, calibration and competition results are outcomes of the team’s combined work.
Team result & limits
The project reports a 93.28% F1 score for tuned AdaBoost on the Kaggle competition test set. This is a competition result on synthetic data, not clinical validation or evidence of real-world diagnostic performance.

Doctoral research · Collaborative publications

MYOBOLICA

Bayesian inference · MCMC · MATLAB · Biomechanics

Many muscle activation patterns can produce the same movement. Which are plausible, and how uncertain are the estimates?

Technical approach
Model muscle recruitment as an inverse problem, combine movement-derived evidence with physiological prior assumptions, and explore posterior distributions using Markov chain Monte Carlo sampling.
My contribution
My doctoral research developed Bayesian models and computational methods for muscle recruitment and uncertainty quantification. It forms part of the scientific foundation for the collaborative MYOBOLICA work.
Research outcome
A dissertation and coauthored studies examine plausible recruitment patterns and variability in muscle control. These are computational research contributions; potential clinical applications require further validation.

Co-first-author paper · Collaborative research

Quantum Pixel Representation and Compression

Quantum algorithms · Circuit synthesis · Image encoding

How can multidimensional images be encoded with fewer quantum circuit resources?

Technical approach
QPIXL unifies several image representations and uses efficiently synthesized rotation and controlled-NOT circuits. Compression acts on transformed image data to reduce state-preparation gate counts.
My contribution
As co-first author, I jointly conducted and analyzed computational experiments evaluating quantum image representations and compression methods. The framework, implementation and reported experiments are collaborative outputs.
Published result & limits
The paper reports gate-count reductions of up to 90% in compression experiments for example scientific images using FRQI encoding while retaining prominent image features. This is an experimental circuit-resource result, not a universal storage reduction or a demonstrated end-to-end quantum speedup.

ChloroFish

SEAMAP data · Model comparison · Prediction intervals

Our team studied links between environmental conditions, chlorophyll measurements and fishery measures using Southeast Area Monitoring and Assessment Program data.

Approach & my contribution
The team compared regression and machine learning methods, including boosting and neural networks, and explored quantile-based prediction intervals for shrimp catch. I contributed to data preparation for shrimp population modeling, including a modified shrimp dataset.
Team evidence
The model-comparison notebook reports validation MSE of 2.4884 for a training-mean baseline and 1.3901 for histogram gradient boosting without feature imputation, predicting the dataset’s total-shrimp-weight target. These results belong to that random holdout evaluation, not an independently tested forecast of future fisheries.

Erdős Institute Deep Learning Boot Camp team project and New Atlantis Labs Ocean Data Fellowship.

Team project · Population-level analysis

Food Environment Atlas

USDA data · Regression · Classification

Our team examined how food access and socioeconomic indicators relate to county-level diabetes prevalence, using the USDA Food Environment Atlas and 2013 prevalence as the target.

Technical approach
Exploratory analysis and feature selection informed comparisons of regression and classification models, including linear methods, random forests and gradient boosting.
Contribution & outcome
I contributed to the team’s analytical notebooks and presentation of findings. The team’s analysis identified predictive associations at county level. These observational patterns do not establish individual risk or causal effects.

Team: Mercy Amankwah, Danielle Brager, Nicole Bruce, Monalisa Dutta and Cyril Dennis Enyi.
